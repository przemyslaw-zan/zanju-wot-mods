# Zanju's Directives Helper

![Mod showcase image](./images/mod.png)

### This mod makes fitting directives quick, and warns you before auto-resupply spends anything.

- **Quick directives access** — directives can be installed and taken off your tank with a single click, without entering directives menu.
- **Only what is needed** — unlike the game's menu, the directives which have no effect are filtered out.
- **Effects at a glance** — directives are split into sections depending on their effect: boosting equipment, boosting fully trained perks and boosting untrained perks to 100%. In the last section you are even told how much each boost gives you depending on your crew training level.
- **Watch your bonds** — if auto-resupply is active and you're on your last directive, the mod will warn you that going into the battle will spend resources.
- **Safe purchase** — if you don't own a directive, the *Show unowned* checkbox shows it on the list. Clicking it will open the game's own purchase window with confirmation — you still have to confirm the purchase, so _misclicks are safe_.
- **Auto-resupply** — can be toggled on or off right in the mod's UI.
- **Dynamic UI** — the window can be collapsed and expanded, moved around, and its width manually changed at will.
