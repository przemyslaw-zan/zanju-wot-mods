Changelog
=========

## 0.1.0 (9 August 2026)

- Initial version. Removes the fixed shell-spread term from the gun marker of twin-gun
  vehicles in Salvo Fire mode, so the reticle shows dispersion only, the way it does on
  every other vehicle.
- Verified in game on an FV229 Contender against client 2.3.1.1: the stock circle measured
  3.90x true size across 0-10 m and 1.81x across 10-25 m, peaking at 7.1x on a 3.4 m aim
  point. The offset is removed on every tick it appears, and is untouched everywhere else.
- Not published: versions below `1.0.0` are never released.
