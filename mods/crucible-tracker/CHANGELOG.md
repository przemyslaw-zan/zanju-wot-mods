Changelog

## 1.1.1 (12 November 2026)

- New Simplified Chinese translation. Thank you [@becausemadoka](https://github.com/becausemadoka)!
- Updated Russian translation. Thank you [@AVPromo](https://github.com/AVPromo)!

## 1.1.0 (5 November 2026)

- New setting: the battle panel can show the progress as a round dial. The dial has the condition icon in its middle, the time limit on top and the numbers below it, with the condition text on its right. Without the mission details, the panel shows only the dials and is only as wide as they need.
- New setting: the battle panel can show the current and the required value, or only the current value.
- The progress bar no longer changes its width while the panel counts, and all progress bars in the panel have the same width.
- The time limit is now white, and turns yellow in its last minute.
- A condition that can no longer be met is now red, not gray.
- The settings window now shows the author and the version of the mod, with a link to the page for bug reports.
- The download now ships Aslain's Mod Menu 2.1.12, in place of 2.1.04.
- Updated for World of Tanks 2.4.1.1.
