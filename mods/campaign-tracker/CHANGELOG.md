Changelog
=========

## Unreleased

- Updated Simplified Chinese translation. Thank you [@becausemadoka](https://github.com/becausemadoka)!

## 1.3.2 (5 November 2026)

- The settings window now shows the author and the version of the mod, with a link to the page for bug reports.
- The download now ships Aslain's Mod Menu 2.1.12, in place of 2.1.04.
- Updated for World of Tanks 2.4.1.1.

## 1.3.1 (27 October 2026)

- Updated Russian translation. Thank you [@AVPromo](https://github.com/AVPromo)!

## 1.3.0 (25 October 2026)

- New setting: the banners can stand on the left or the right side of the vehicle name.
- The banners no longer take one of the game's own garage views, so they can no longer block another mod, or be blocked by one.
- The download now ships Aslain's Mod Menu 2.1.04, for the new in-game settings.
- Settings moved to `%APPDATA%\Wargaming.net\WorldOfTanks\mods\Zanju\campaign-tracker\`. On its first start, the mod copies them from the old folder, `%APPDATA%\zanju_wot_mods_cache\campaign-tracker\`, so nothing is lost.

## 1.2.3 (14 October 2026)

- Updated for World of Tanks 2.4.1.0.

## 1.2.2 (2 October 2026)

- New Simplified Chinese translation. Thank you [@becausemadoka](https://github.com/becausemadoka)!

## 1.2.1 (1 October 2026)

- Fixed missing letters in the banner tooltips, in languages that the game draws with a different font (e.g. Chinese).
- Updated for World of Tanks 2.4.0.2.

## 1.2.0 (11 September 2026)

- A campaign screen opened from a banner now has the game's own back button. It walks back the way the game does: to the campaign, then to the campaign map. Escape now takes the same path instead of closing straight to the garage.
- Banners now show progress on missions that you have to complete in several different vehicles.
- Banner icons now carry the colour of what they mean: green for result improvements and order retrievals, yellow for locked vehicles and paused missions.
- Fixed the game's reward and event windows never opening. The notice about missed events kept coming back, and its button did nothing.
- Fixed Escape, Tab and the radial menu commands sometimes doing nothing.
- Fixed the mod slowing the garage down the longer the game ran.
- New Russian and Ukrainian translations. Thank you [@ICELUV0x](https://github.com/ICELUV0x)!

## 1.1.1 (2 September 2026)

- Fixed the banner not working for the Fossa operation.
- The banners now show for the random battles only.

## 1.1.0 (2 September 2026)

- Moved banner tooltips into a Gameface window of their own, on a higher window layer. They now draw over the game's native windows, such as the platoon window.
- Fixed the banners not appearing in non-default garages.
- Updated for World of Tanks 2.4.

## 1.0.0 (28 August 2026)

- Initial release of the mod.
