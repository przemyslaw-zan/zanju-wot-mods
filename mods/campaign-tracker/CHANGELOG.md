Changelog
=========

## 0.1.0 (25 August 2026)

- First iteration. Each active personal missions campaign gets its own garage badge, fixed beside the vehicle name and experience block.
- The badge shows the campaign number over a short mission id such as `LT-1` or `UN-10`.
- Hover a badge for the operation, the full mission name, and the progress of every condition.
- Click a badge to open the game's own screen for that mission.
- Shift + Click a badge to pause its mission, or to resume a paused one. Ctrl + Click resets it, through the game's own confirmation dialog. The card lists what each click does and lights the line for the keys you hold. Only Object 279 (e) accepts these two actions, because the game allows them on no other operation.
- Some missions must be completed a number of times inside a limited run of battles. The badge gives the successes so far, the number needed, and the battles left. The card gives the requirement, and one mark per battle.
- Other missions must reach a total inside a limited run of battles. The badge gives the total so far, the target, and how many battles are left. It also says whether you keep up. Such a mission asks for one constant average a battle, and the card gives where you stand against that average as a percentage, where 100% is exactly on it. The badge tints the total green or red to match.
- Others again must be completed a set number of times, in a row or in any order. The badge gives what is in hand against what it takes.
- Some missions must be completed in several different vehicles. The card counts how many are still wanted, and names the vehicles already locked out.
- The badge shows a small icon for the two states that stop a tank from making progress: a pause icon while the mission is on pause, and a lock icon when the tank in the garage is already spent on a mission that wants several different vehicles.
